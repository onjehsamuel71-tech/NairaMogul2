# 🇳🇬 NAIRA MOGUL: Empire Builder

> From the trenches to Banana Island. Build your Nigerian business empire, dodge Sapa, manage your street cred, and flex your net worth on the leaderboard!

---

## 🚀 About the Game
**NAIRA MOGUL** is a frontend-first, high-stakes Nigerian hustle and fintech strategy simulation game. Starting with just **₦100,000** capital, players must navigate real-world economic survival, buy income-generating assets across iconic Lagos districts, survive random "Sapa" dilemmas, and climb the ranks from a local street hustler to an elite oil baron.

## ✨ Core Features
- **Passive Income Hustle Loop:** Buy and manage businesses like POS Kiosks, Suya Stands, Danfo fleets, and luxury real estate that generate income second-by-second.
- **Open-World Districts Map:** Unlock and progress through distinct hustle zones—from **Mushin & Agege** up to **Lekki & Banana Island**.
- **Random "Sapa" & Dilemma Events:** Unpredictable pop-ups that force strategic financial choices (generator breakdowns, police wahala, or unexpected cash alerts).
- **Flex Garage & Brag Cards:** Showcase your wealth by purchasing luxury cars and generating shareable status cards.
- **Pure Frontend Architecture:** Built locally with React and Tailwind CSS, featuring local browser saving with zero backend database dependencies required[cite: 2].
